# Phase 81A Checkpoint L6.6 - Ready-Successor Targeting

## Verdict

`REFINE: target_eligibility`. The candidate was rejected and removed; it is not
product behavior.

## Population

- profile: `phase81a-ready-successor-l6-6-7x10`;
- `7` paired worlds, `10` seasons, exactly `7` workers;
- identical seed prefix and footballers to L6.5;
- candidate: bounded within-club succession order plus a soft preference for an
  exact-role player aged `21-29` above the incumbent-role quality floor.

## Result

The candidate arm was byte-identical to the bounded L6.5 arm in all seven
worlds. Prime-age acquisition share stayed `0.164604`, career-generated
prime-age share stayed `0.021040`, local replacement stayed `0.054945`, and
season-ten generated-leader share stayed `0.245238`. Every incremental delta
was zero.

The result is not evidence that age-aware recruitment has no value. It proves
that the viable and affordable target pool supplied no candidate on which this
preference could act. Repeatedly changing the preference threshold would tune a
branch absent from the ten-season population.

## Product Consequence

No ready-successor tier survives. L6.4's direct career facts instead become the
next owner: opening seniors who remain leaders at season ten are, in every
world, stronger at age `33-34` than they were at opening age `23-24` (world
means `+0.243` to `+0.964`). Supply, academy minutes and development parity were
already healthy. The next checkpoint therefore tests a softer, earlier decline
curve while preserving exceptional veterans.
