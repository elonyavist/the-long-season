## Interpretation

The result confirms that authored ceiling supply matters: adding potential
raises generated leadership by `6.90` percentage points and moves both
stationarity measures in the intended direction. It also shows that a
state-conditioned annual quota is the wrong product boundary. It modifies the
future vacancy/promotion state that writes the next intake, so later cohorts
are no longer the same population; and the median-of-seven static targets are
too low for many per-world role references, leaving the generation share at
only `0.4232`.

The next candidate must author a stable routine-youth ceiling distribution at
the individual generation boundary, independent of annual candidate counts.
Its purity check must distinguish the immediate authored fact from legitimate
later career consequences, and it must retain the same material downstream
thresholds rather than treating a higher potential count as success.
