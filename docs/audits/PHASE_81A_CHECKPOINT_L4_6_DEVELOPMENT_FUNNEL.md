# Phase 81A Checkpoint L4.6 - Development Funnel Retry

## Decision

**REFINE with a narrower owner.** The corrected realization curve reached
development parity in all `7/7` worlds and reduced active opening-senior
survival below the frozen `0.60` ceiling, but it did not make career-generated
players own the season-ten leaderboards. The exact ledger assigned the next
correction to dated match load rather than hiding it inside another growth
coefficient.

The historical L4.3 generation signature is intentionally not an L4.6 gate.
L4.5 changed the generation owner under a separately measured structural `GO`,
so requiring the superseded byte signature would reject the authorized input
while saying nothing about development.

## Attribution

The retry removed the second use of remaining potential room as a rate
multiplier while retaining it as the exact no-overshoot ceiling. A controlled
real-player-shaped test proved zero room remains zero, ordinary prospects stop
at their own potential and high-room prospects remain bounded by the monthly
rate. The season-ten ledger then showed opening players aged `33+` still taking
more than four million minutes. That observation assigned the next correction
to dated match load and recovery, never directly to goals or assists.

## Limitation

This checkpoint establishes that generated potential can become senior-level
ability. Its `REFINE` authorized only the already-observed match-load owner; it
did not establish a credible age distribution among top performers. That
question belongs to the integrated L5 population.
