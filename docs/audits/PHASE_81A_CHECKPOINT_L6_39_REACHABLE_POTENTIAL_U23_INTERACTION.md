# Phase 81A Checkpoint L6.39 - Reachable Potential x U23 Participation

## Verdict

**`STOP / RETHINK`.** Truthful reachable potential and canonical U23
participation do not form a credible successor pipeline when combined. The
pair raises the common serious cohort's mean maximum current quality, but it
reduces current-16 successors, does not increase generated leadership and
leaves the late-career leaderboard far outside the frozen product bands.

The two candidates, their callable profile and every analysis seam were
removed. The shipped product is exactly the pre-step product.

control arm's seven First Divisions, season ten contains about `92` opening-
population current-16 seniors against only `16` career-generated current-16
players. The generated current-16 group averages about `30.7` starts and
`2,675` minutes, so selection is not withholding the successors that exist.

try another growth multiplier, U23 schedule or age-conditioned output rule. It
must separate:

1. survival of the opening high-quality population;
2. annual production of high-quality successors;
3. the national exceptional-stock vacancy/allocation loop;
4. opportunity after a successor already reaches senior quality.

Only the demonstrated owner may receive a correction. No direct age read in
lineup selection, goals or assists is authorized, and no synthetic external
