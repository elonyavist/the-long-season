## Delivered Model

- Exceptional slots are selected before generation and receive
  archetype-compatible profiles.
- Initial and annual rarity diagnostics measure real stored current/potential
  outcomes rather than forced IDs.
- `Player.potential` remains the sole stored ceiling.
- The engine derives a public age- and role-aware lower/expected/P90-upper
  projection bounded by that ceiling.
- Squad, Market, and player profiles render a six-slot half-star range with
  patterned uncertain upside and accessible range text.
- Public value prices the public projection with uncertainty discounting,
  shared upper-tail compression, whole-euro quantization, and a rare eligible
  `EUR 150m` clamp.
- Stored ceiling and public upper remain separate diagnostic facts.
- Asking, offered, countered, agreed, and completed fees remain distinct and
  observable.
