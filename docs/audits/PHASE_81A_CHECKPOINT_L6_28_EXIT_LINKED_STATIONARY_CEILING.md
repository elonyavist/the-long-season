## Consequence

Real senior exits are not a representative source for future senior-quality
ceilings: leavers disproportionately encode the tail of a career rather than a
stationary replacement target. The candidate and every production/analysis
seam introduced for it were removed in the same step. L6.29 must measure the
annual cohort's ceiling distribution against the opening like-aged population;
it may not revive a generic probability, global uplift or exit inheritance.
