# Phase 81A Checkpoint L6.38 - Shared Reachable Potential

## Verdict

**`STOP / RETHINK`.** One shared age/family reachability table fixes the
generation/runtime contradiction but does not fix generational renewal. It
reduces the realised quality tail and makes the scorer/creator quality rungs
older. The candidate is rejected and removed.

- profile: `phase81a-shared-reachable-room-l6-38-7x10`;
- two paired arms, seven worlds, ten seasons, exactly seven workers;
- opening world identity held in `7/7`;
- first divergence was potential-only for the same player/month in `7/7`;
- common serious support `30`, rare support `8`;

The cached rebuild corrected one diagnostic assumption without touching the
simulation: content's shared table is looser through 21 but can be tighter at a
later age, so purity is “potential-only difference”, not a required sign. A
focused test covers both legal directions.

## Owner Learned From The Pair

The shared policy truthfully preserves active young exceptional ceilings. That
changes annual stock allocation even though candidate IDs and generation-time
current quality remain paired:

| class | legacy count | shared count | legacy/shared mean max current |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| routine | `1,128` | `1,150` | `11.2870 / 11.5093` |
| interesting | `509` | `517` | `11.7936 / 12.5893` |
| serious | `179` | `187` | `12.1251 / 13.0373` |
| rare | `63` | `25` | `15.3807 / 16.2548` |

The old runtime bug compressed six-star ceilings while players were still in
the age-15-to-20 national stock. That created artificial vacancies and explains
why legacy generated more rare prospects. Restoring those vacancies would be
neither truthful nor sufficient: even with `63` rare prospects, the old product
still had only `17` generated current-16 players and old leaderboards.

The remaining transition is concrete. In the shared arm, represented serious
prospects at ages 20-21 average current quality about `12.7` while retaining
potential about `16.2`; by age 23 current is only `13.32`. The potential is now
real, but it is not converted into senior quality soon enough. A later step may
test that interaction only through canonical football activity and the
existing growth rules. It may not add an age/output bonus, increase rare stock
to compensate for the removed bug, or claim this rejected policy as product.
