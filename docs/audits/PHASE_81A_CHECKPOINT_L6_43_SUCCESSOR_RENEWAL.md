as product behaviour. The normal CLI and web path retains pre-Step-16L
semantics; the candidate remains callable only by the L6.43A analysis profile.

## Result

The candidate made `728` exact-five assignments over `70` world-seasons, but:

- generated season-ten current-16 stock reached opening-senior stock in `0/7`
  worlds;
- candidate current-16 stock exceeded control in only `2/7` worlds;
- pooled career-generated scorer/creator leader share was `0.2071` against the
  frozen `0.50` floor;
- its funnel was `1876 generated -> 1089 senior-observed -> 24 ceiling-16 ->
  17 current-16 -> 16 retained -> 5 leaders`, against six control leaders.

Reconciliation, selected-player semantics, total-stock protection and all
unrelated integrated gates held. Age-33-plus leadership remained reachable.
Six-star assignment identities drifted, so the new five-star lane was not
isolated from the existing rarity lane.

## Decision

The result rejects the policy, but does not identify where assigned prospects
are lost. Checkpoint L6.43A therefore traces the selected IDs through academy,
senior registration, minutes, realised ability and leadership before any new
gameplay change.
