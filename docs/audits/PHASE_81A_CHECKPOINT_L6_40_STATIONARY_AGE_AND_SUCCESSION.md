and only `15` career-generated players. Opening seniors are at least half of
that rung in `7/7` worlds; the per-world share ranges from `0.7143` to `1.0000`.

The linked role-current paths explain why the opening stock remains strong:

- at age `30..32`, the one-season median role-current delta is exactly `0` for
  every one of the ten roles;
- at age `33+`, the weighted one-season median remains only about `0` to
  `-0.08` depending on role;

The next correction owner is therefore the shared aging policy, not a direct
age penalty in selection, goals or assists. It must differentiate physical
decline by football role while allowing technical/mental compensation and
exceptional longevity.
