# Phase 81A - Amendment A12: Option-B Post-Match Boundary

## Product Decision

Phase 81A does not add a post-match preparation action to the smaller
own-squad option-B MVP accepted by Amendment A11.

The shipped loop is deliberately complete at these boundaries:

1. before each match, the AI selects its best legal formation, XI and tactical
   plan from its own available players and current condition;
2. during the match, it may react to score, time, fatigue, injury and dismissal;
3. after the match, durable tactical chapters explain the canonical football
   facts and survive reload, but do not alter a future match.

This is a deferral, not evidence that the historical preparation experiment
passed. The former recovery/rehearsal/study targets are superseded because two
of those actions require product systems the accepted MVP removed.

## Why No Placeholder Is Shipped

- A plan-rehearsal bonus would recreate a rejected third execution candidate,
  direct formation bonus or hidden strength multiplier.
- Opponent study would reopen the delayed-evidence and manager/AI parity design
  that option B explicitly deferred.
- Recovery versus development would overlap the established day-recovery and
  monthly-development owners. It needs a separate paired attribution before a
  new player choice can own either outcome.
- A stored action with no consequence would be dead product code.

Formation history remains durable data with no production AI reader. A future
opponent-aware feature may consume it only through a separately frozen,
manager-visible latency contract.

## Checkpoint Consequence

Checkpoint E now asks whether option B remains coherent across multiple
fixtures and a reload boundary:

- catalog AI remains opponent-blind and fallback-free;
- current fitness, recent load, injury and suspension create real lineup
  rotation without changing the product into opponent scouting;
- score/time/fatigue/injury/dismissal response paths remain reachable;
- committed tactical facts and chapters survive JSON, SQLite and web reload;
- no second storage reset, result change or RNG realignment occurs.

Step 16 must report the absent post-match gameplay choice as an MVP limitation.
It must not retain the obsolete preparation or `OpponentRead` gates as failures
inside the new acceptance decision.
