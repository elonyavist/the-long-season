# Phase 81A Checkpoint L6.22 — Academy Intake Factorial

## Decision

`STOP / RETHINK`. The bounded First-Division interesting-frequency and
five-star-ceiling factors are rejected together and individually. All product
and analysis code introduced for the experiment was removed.

## Population

- four arms on the exact seven L6.20 world seeds and ten seasons;
- exactly seven workers for both fresh arms;
- `00` current, `10` frequency-only, `01` ceiling-only, `11` combined;
- zero reconciliation failures;
- two byte-identical cache-only evaluator artifacts;
- final SHA-256:
  `86c06696593beab6ca88198b96ed9de251b0bf69e06ccdee86bdac9409e35204`.

## Result

Frequency alone changes generated-leader share by `+0.0024`; ceiling alone by
`+0.0071`. Combined, they change it by `-0.0238`, leaving only `0.2476` of
season-ten leaders career-generated. The factorial interaction is `-0.0333`.
Interesting leaders fall `17 -> 15`, mature quality improves only `0.0215`, and
stored-ceiling insufficiency improves only `0.0098`.

The combined arm also moves one Third-Division generated player between routine
and interesting through downstream career evolution, violating the frozen
zero-spillover gate. This makes the formal decision structural, but does not
cause the product rejection: every renewal target is independently red.

## Handoff

The engine does not lack a simple quantity of interesting or five-star academy
players. More of both produces fewer leaders. The next checkpoint must follow
the same generated IDs from authored current/ceiling through minutes,
development, retention and season-ten leadership. No new frequency, ceiling,
growth, minutes, market or aging coefficient is authorized until that paired
lifecycle attribution identifies its owner.
