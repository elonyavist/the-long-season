# Phase 81A - Amendment A11: Smaller Own-Squad MVP Acceptance

## Product Decision

Accept product option B for the current MVP after the two execution candidates
failed their frozen magnitude gates:

- before every match the AI chooses the formation, eleven and tactical plan
  that best fit its own available players and their current condition;
- the AI does not read the opponent before the match;
- automatic in-match decisions may react to score, time, fatigue, injury and
  dismissal because those are live match facts;
- opponent-aware pre-match AI remains a future extension and may only consume
  the same delayed evidence available to the manager;
- no direct formation bonus, hidden result estimate, strength multiplier or
  third plan-execution candidate enters this phase.

## Evidence Boundary

This is an explicit product acceptance, not a retrospective gate change.
Checkpoint D2 remains `REFINE`, Step 12F remains `REFINE` with its candidate
removed, and Step 12H remains `STOP / RETHINK` with its candidate removed.

The shipped selector already establishes:

- varied own-squad policies, all three lateral focuses and no universal shape;
- zero opponent-source reads and deterministic catalog-order invariance;
- positive correct-fit and negative deliberate-mismatch mean point directions
  in both D2 sets, with only about `0.70..0.79` points of seasonal separation;
- canonical route, player-task, xG and result paths with no artificial bonus.

That effect is smaller than the former `>=3`-point separation and must be
described as small. UI or explanation code may show the plan and the football
facts it produced; it may not claim a causal point gain, guaranteed superiority
or optimal response to the opponent.

## Consequence

Step 13 may open to explain canonical match chapters. It changes no gameplay
and cannot manufacture agency in prose. Steps 14-16 may finish persistence,
post-match preparation and integrated validation. The final report must retain
the tactical magnitude debt as an explicit limitation and separately evaluate
the already-green L6.31 generational-renewal candidate.
