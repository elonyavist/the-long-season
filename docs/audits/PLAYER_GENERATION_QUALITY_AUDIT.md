# Player Generation Quality Audit

Date: 2026-06-21
Phase: `24-player-generation-quality-rework`
Step: `01-current-generator-audit`

## Summary

The user concern is confirmed.

The current generator is deterministic and already good at fictional identity variety, but its ability model is too broad and too uniform for a third-division career game. A single base value drives almost every attribute, and role adjustments are small. That means stronger third-division clubs can produce players with credible role-primary attributes but also inflated off-role attributes.

## Confirmed Current Behavior

- The current fake league is explicitly third division:
  - `generateFakeClubs()` creates clubs with `category: "third_division"`.
  - `createFakeLeagueSystem()` creates `Demo Third Division`.
- Player current ability is driven by one broad `base` formula:
  - club hierarchy;
  - slot noise;
  - small seed variance;
  - archetype current-ability offset.
- Potential is generated as `base + archetype.potentialUplift`, capped at `20`.
- Position-specific attributes are generated by `abilitiesForPosition(base, position)`.
- `abilitiesForPosition` applies only small offsets around the same base:
  - attackers get finishing bonus, but still keep relatively high tackling;
  - defenders get tackling/positioning bonus, but still keep relatively high finishing, penalties, free kicks, and goalkeeper values;
  - goalkeepers get goalkeeper bonuses, but still keep outfield values close to base.
- Archetypes already separate age/current/potential in a first-pass way, but they are not division-specific and do not enforce a league-level rarity budget.
- Identity generation now varies by seed and nationality distribution is credible enough for this phase. The player-generation issue is technical quality, not name variation.

## Observed Suspicious Output

Inspection seed: `world-a`.

Examples from current generated third-division output:

- `player:province-01-05` (`lb`) has finishing around `13.39`, penalties around `14.39`, goalkeeper reflexes around `10.39`, and tackling around `16.39`.
- `player:province-01-11` (`st`) has finishing around `16.08`, tackling around `13.08`, goalkeeper reflexes around `10.08`, and potential finishing around `17.08`.
- `player:province-02-15` (`cm`) has passing around `17.04`, tackling around `14.04`, penalties around `15.04`, goalkeeper reflexes around `11.04`, and goalkeeper footwork around `13.04`.
- Multiple non-goalkeepers have goalkeeper reflexes above `8`.
- Multiple defenders in the top clubs have finishing or penalties above `12`.

These values are not necessarily bugs for a single rare player, but they are too common because they come from the common base formula, not from explicit rarity rules.

## Risk Assessment

High product risk before deeper career systems:

- Lower divisions can feel overpowered.
- Attribute screens will feel less believable once the UI exposes full player cards.
- Market and scouting will inherit noisy value signals.
- Prospects may become too good too early because potential is derived from the same broad current base.
- Role identity is weak: players look like generalist stat blocks instead of footballers shaped by position.

## Step 02 Requirements

- Add explicit generation bands by division and club tier.
- Keep third-division current-ability bands materially lower than second and first division.
- Separate current-ability band data from potential band data.
- Preserve deterministic output by seed.

## Step 03 Requirements

- Replace small role offsets with explicit role templates.
- Add hard caps for off-role attributes.
- Give goalkeepers a distinct path.

## Step 04 Requirements

- Expand age/current/potential archetypes.
- Keep lower-division high-potential players raw unless they are explicitly rare.

## Step 05 Requirements

- Add deterministic league-level rarity budgets.
- Do not guarantee a white-fly player per club.
