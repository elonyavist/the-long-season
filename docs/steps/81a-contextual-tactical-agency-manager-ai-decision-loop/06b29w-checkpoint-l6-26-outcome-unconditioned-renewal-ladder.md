## Frozen Outcome-Unconditioned Ranks

Build exactly ten slots per world, competition and lane for each rung, using
player ID as the final deterministic tie-breaker:

1. `quality`: current ability among players in lane-eligible roles;
2. `opportunity_rate`: shots or creator nominations per 900 minutes among
   players with at least 900 minutes;
3. `raw_opportunity`: shots or creator nominations;
4. `club_expected_output`: opportunity count multiplied by the player's
   leave-one-out club conversion rate — club goals excluding that player over
   club shots excluding him, or club assists excluding him over creator
   nominations excluding him;
5. `actual_output`: canonical goals or assists.

Every rung reports career-generated slots, opening slots and unknown-origin
violations, pooled and per world. A transfer row remains one player-season fact
at its canonical closing club; no reconstruction or double count is allowed.

Leave-one-out denominators equal to zero are `not_observed` and structural if
they prevent ten expected-output ranks in any competition-lane. Expected
output is diagnostic only and never written into match or player state.
