## Thesis

The two rejected factors answered different halves of one transition and have
never been tested together:

- L6.36 supplied canonical low-detail U23 football, but the current runtime had
  already compressed serious ceilings to about `13.4`; extra minutes could not
  grow beyond missing room;
- L6.38 preserved serious potential near `16.2`, but those players still
  averaged only about `12.7` current quality at ages 20-21 and `13.32` at 23;
  the shared-only arm made both quality rungs `0.77` years older.

This step tests the interaction, not either rejected single factor again. A
credible successor needs both a truthful reachable ceiling and canonical
football activity. Neither potential nor age may grant output, selection or
free growth.

## Frozen U23 Factor

Use the exact football structure preregistered in Step 16E, without changing
its volume after seeing L6.39:

- player is on an active senior roster, not in that club's academy, and age
  18-22 at the exact month-end checkpoint;
- at most two stable low-detail 90-minute fixtures in an active competition
  month;
- real senior minutes replace whole fixtures:

```text
fixtures = max(0, 2 - floor(real senior minutes / 90))
```

- the canonical participation ledger owns idempotency, attribution,
  development, adaptation and reload;
- these facts have no result, rating, goal, assist, injury or condition event;
- a player cannot receive academy and development-squad facts in the same
  month.

This approximates reserve/U23 football until loans and reserve competitions
exist. It is not a youth bonus: zero canonical activity remains zero growth.

## Decision And Removal Rule

- `GO_CLOSE`: combined clears materiality, coherence, absolute bands and every
  guardrail. Ship combined as the sole product and close the finding.
- `GO_MATERIAL_CONTINUE`: combined clears every interaction/materiality rule
  and guardrail but at least one absolute band remains red. Ship the proven
  structural pair, remove all switches, then attribute only the residual.
- `REFINE_INTERACTION`: combined beats control and both singles in all four
  primary directions in at least `5/7`, interaction terms are positive and no
  guardrail regresses, but a preregistered magnitude misses. A next checkpoint
  may inspect only conversion timing; it may not tune from leaderboard output.
- `STOP / RETHINK`: purity/reconciliation failure, reversal, one single arm
  matching the combined arm, non-positive interaction, lost veteran
  reachability, or a new inherited failure.

After the decision, no rejected callable profile, flag, reference function,
label, fixture producer or evaluator remains. If combined ships, it becomes the
only runtime path; otherwise the exact pre-step product is restored. No HTML is
