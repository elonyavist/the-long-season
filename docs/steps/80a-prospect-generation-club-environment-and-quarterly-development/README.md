## Locked Decisions

- Development uses monthly participation facts but applies them in one
  quarterly state transition plus season-end residual flush.
- No permanent hidden realization modifier or stored development destination.
- Public potential is current + statistical `P50` + reachable upper.
- Outfield upper is full through age 20, narrows at 21-27, and equals current
  at 28+; goalkeepers retain uncertainty longer and equal current at 32+.
- After age 20, projection factors are calibrated for every completed age and
  role family; a flat `21..24` implementation band is forbidden. The visible
  `20 -> 21` boundary remains measurable because full upper through 20 is an
  explicit product rule.
- Generated age-`15..20` `interesting`, `serious`, and `rare_prodigy` lanes
  own at least one stored star of room through one ceiling-first joint-profile
  owner. Routine players may plateau, and stored room is not a guaranteed
  development outcome.
- Dynamic 18-club tiers are `4 / 4 / 6 / 4`, recomputed at rollover and frozen
  for the season.
- Reputation has no history ledger and moves by at most two points per year.
- Seven environment states use the exact accepted category/tier matrix.
- Young ceiling-`3.5+` calibration bands are `4..8%` Serie C, `8..15%` Serie
  B, and `15..25%` Serie A.
- The national active stock is `2..3` established current-six players and
  `4..5` age-15-to-20 ceiling-six prospects, with at most one outside Serie A
  and at most one per club.
- Public value prices achieved current, a discounted `current -> P50` tranche,
  and a smaller positive `P50 -> upper` option tranche. Better public upside
  cannot reduce value; upper is not guaranteed; `marketContext` and the global
  uncertainty haircut are removed; the one global `€150m` cap and context
  invariance remain.
- Phase 79C division-value tolerances and the Phase 79D `302 / 100 worlds`
  stored-six baseline are historical, non-comparable evidence; Step 01 freezes
  a new source-backed calibration epoch before implementation.
- Live AI market decisions use the same public projection as the manager.
- The existing public-assessment Module becomes the only live Interface for
  UI, sorting, value, willingness, and AI. Stored ceiling stays behind
  generation/development/projection/diagnostic seams.
- Club development environment is derived from season-frozen category/tier
  facts and stamped policy; it is not a second mutable or historical save fact.
- Quarterly orchestration reuses ordered monthly participation rows and their
  closed-month keys; no duplicate quarterly ledger is persisted.
- Incompatible beta saves are deleted; no migration branches are retained.
